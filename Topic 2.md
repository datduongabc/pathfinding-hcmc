# Pahtfinding HCMC

**Language:** Python

## TOPIC: Pathfinding in Ho Chi Minh City

Construct a map of at least **100 points of interest** in HCMC.

Defining a heuristic that is a function of vehicle density, travel speed, or flow rate… In other words, the heuristic value at a point of interest is NOT a constant, but rather a function of different inputs (time-variant, constants, etc.).

Based on the collected data, propose the shortest/cheapest path from Point A to Point B in HCMC using **A-star search**.
